Six Numbers. REAL DATA.

every stroke. in real time.

01

Face Angle at Impact

Responsible for 85-90% of where your putt starts. One degree open or closed,  and a six-footer slides past the cup.

02

Putting Tempo

Your back-to-forward-stroke ratio on every putt. This is where distance control breaks down.

03

Loft Angle

OnePuttPro shows the exact loft you are delivering, and reads it live at address.

04

Lie Angle Change

A move too small to see in the mirror, now captured as an exact number.

05

Acceleration at Impact

The most common mistake in putting. This confirms whether you are hitting through the ball or braking before contact.

06

Impact Position

Impact position affects the roll direction even when face angle looks fine. OPP tells you exactly where contact happened, every time.
